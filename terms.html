Overview

Declutter Pawn Shop ("DPS", "we", "us") runs a digital marketplace for pre-owned goods across Lagos, Nigeria. Buyers find things they want at fair prices. Sellers turn idle items into cash. We handle the escrow, the review, and the messy middle.

By using DPS — whether you're browsing, listing, negotiating, or paying — you agree to these Terms. If anything here doesn't sit right with you, the fix is simple: don't use the platform. If you keep using it, we take that as your acceptance.

These Terms apply to the website, the Telegram channel, Dayo (our bot), Dina (our Instagram automation for Partners), and any related services we operate.

03

Our role

DPS is an intermediary. We connect buyers and sellers. We review listings, hold funds in escrow, and provide the tools that make the transaction safe. We are not the buyer, and we are not the seller.

We don't own the items listed. We don't physically take custody of them. We don't arrange logistics beyond connecting the two parties. Physical transfer happens directly between buyer and seller, at pickup.

What we do guarantee: money in escrow is handled according to these Terms, listings go through human review before they're published, and payouts are processed to verified bank details.

04

Listing standards

Every listing is reviewed by our team before it goes live. Approval isn't guaranteed, and we may reject any listing for any reason — usually because it's unclear, mispriced, or missing useful detail.

Every listing must include

  • Real photos of the actual item. Not stock images, not manufacturer renders, not photos of a similar item.
  • An honest description. Condition, what's included, and any flaws. Scratches, dents, missing accessories — say so.
  • A realistic price. Priced for a pre-owned Lagos market, not aspirational pricing.
  • A pickup location. Mainland or Island. Buyers need to know where they're going.
Misleading listings get you suspended.If a buyer confirms a purchase and the item doesn't match the listing, escrow reversal is possible — but your account gets flagged. Repeat offenders are removed from the platform.

08

Offers & negotiation

Don't want to pay the asking price? Make an offer. The seller decides whether to accept, counter, or decline.

How it works

  1. You submit an offer with your price and a short note. A ₦300 negotiation fee is charged via Paystack at this point.
  2. The seller has 24 hours to respond. They can accept your offer, counter with a different price, or decline.
  3. If they accept, you get a payment link at your offer price. Pay within the window and the item is yours.
  4. If they counter, you get another 24 hours to respond to the counter — accept, decline, or walk away.
  5. If they decline or the window expires, your offer closes. You can try again if the listing is still live.

You can withdraw your offer

Any time before the seller responds, you can pull your offer. The seller is notified. The ₦300 fee is not refunded.

The ₦300 fee is non-refundable.It covers verification and keeps offers serious. It stays with the platform whether or not the deal completes.

Accepted offers are binding once you complete payment. Walk away after paying without collecting, and you'll be flagged for repeat no-shows.

09

Escrow & payment

All payments run through Paystack, a PCI-compliant processor. We never touch raw card details.

The escrow flow

  1. Buyer pays the full agreed price via Paystack.
  2. Funds land in DPS escrow — not with the seller.
  3. Buyer and seller get each other's contact details. Pickup is arranged directly.
  4. Buyer inspects the item in person. If it doesn't match the listing, the buyer walks away and gets a full refund.
  5. On acceptance, buyer shares their 4-digit PIN. Escrow releases to the seller minus our commission.

Escrow funds are held securely until the transaction completes or a dispute is resolved. Funds are not invested, lent, or used for any purpose other than settlement.

Escrow isn't a bank account.It's a transactional hold. Funds move in and out as deals complete. There's no interest, no account, no withdrawal at will.

Pickup & PIN confirmation

Pickup is arranged directly between buyer and seller. We recommend daytime hours, in a public place, and completing the inspection carefully.

At pickup, the buyer inspects the item. If everything matches, the buyer shares a 4-digit PIN with the seller. That PIN is the escrow release signal.

  • Never share your PIN before you have the item in hand.Not to the seller, not to anyone claiming to be from DPS. Our staff will never ask for your PIN.
  • Once the PIN is entered and confirmed, the transaction is final.No take-backs, no post-pickup refunds.
  • Lost or compromised PIN?Contact us immediately. We can regenerate one and reset your lockout.
DPS never asks for your PIN.Not by phone, email, Telegram, or DM. Anyone requesting it is committing fraud. Report them.

Payouts

Once the buyer's PIN is verified, escrow releases and payout is queued to the seller's bank account on file.

  • Timing:Payouts are processed within 24 hours of PIN verification, typically same-day during banking hours.
  • Destination:The account details on the seller's profile at the time of the sale. Make sure they're correct before you list.
  • Amount:Agreed sale price minus commission. The exact figure is shown before you confirm the sale.
  • Failed payouts:If a payout fails due to incorrect bank details, we contact you. Fixing takes a business day, but the money stays in escrow until then.

We don't hold payouts for longer than necessary. If a payout is delayed beyond the window above and it's not due to a dispute or bank issue on your side, contact us and we'll sort it.

Disputes

Disputes are rare, but they happen. The system is designed so most of them are resolved at pickup, before any money moves.

Before PIN confirmation

  • Item doesn't match the listing?Don't share the PIN. Walk away. You get a full refund.
  • Item isn't working?Same thing. Inspect, test, then decide. Before PIN is your window.
  • Seller is unresponsive?Contact us. We can extend your payment hold while we look into it.

After PIN confirmation

Once the PIN is entered, the transaction is complete and escrow releases. We can't reverse a confirmed sale. If something unexpected surfaces, contact us anyway — we'll see what we can do, but reversal isn't promised.

The PIN exists for this reason.It's your last chance to back out. Once it's used, the sale is done. Treat it like signing a contract.
