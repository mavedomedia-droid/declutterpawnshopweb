How the program works

From your page to your payout.

Six steps, and you are at the first one right now.

01
We review your account You are here

We check your business details and the page you gave us. Nothing lists until this clears, so your stock will not appear on DPS yet. You can sign in to the dashboard in the meantime and look around.

02
Your page gets connected

Once approved, we connect your Instagram page. From then on we check it on a schedule and pick up new posts automatically. You do not upload anything twice.

03
Your items list themselves

New posts become live DPS listings without you lifting a finger, and they drop into the Telegram channel the same way every other item does.

04
You stay in control from the dashboard

Anything we list, you can change. Fix a price we read wrong, correct a description, or pull an item that has already gone. Everything sits in /partners-admin under your login.

05
A buyer pays into escrow

The buyer pays DPS, not you. The money is held until the handover is confirmed, which protects both of you. You get the buyer's contact details and arrange the meetup.

06
Their PIN releases your payout

The buyer holds a 4-digit release PIN. Once they have the item in hand and are happy, they give you the PIN, you enter it, and your payout leaves to your bank.

What DPS takes: your partner rate and how it is applied are set out in the partner terms, and the welcome email confirms the rate on your account.
