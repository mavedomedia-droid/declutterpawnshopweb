How the sale works

From listing to payout.

Seven steps, and your item is at the first one right now.

01
We review your listing You are here

We check that the photos match the item, the description is accurate, and the price is sane for the condition. Most listings clear within a few hours. If something needs fixing we mark it Needs changes and email you exactly what to change, so nothing gets rejected without a reason.

02
It goes live on the marketplace

Your item appears on the items page and drops into our Telegram channel. The link you copied above starts working, and anywhere you share it points straight at your listing.

03
Buyers check it is still available

Before anyone pays, they can send an availability check. You get a message and confirm it is still there. Answer quickly, because an unanswered check is where most sales quietly die.

04
They buy at your price, or they negotiate

Two ways this goes, and you stay in control of both.

They buy

They pay your asking price. Straight to step 05.

They offer

They pay a ₦300 fee to send a serious offer. You get an email and choose: accept, counter, or decline. They can withdraw it before you answer. Nothing moves without your say-so.

05
Payment is held in escrow

The buyer pays DPS, not you. The money sits with us, safe for both sides. Neither of you can touch it yet, which is what makes it safe to meet a stranger.

06
Details shared, you arrange the meetup

Once payment lands in escrow, you and the buyer get each other's contact details and area. Agree on a public place in daylight. Bring the item and let them inspect it properly.

07
Their PIN releases your payout

The buyer holds a 4-digit release PIN. Once they have inspected the item and are happy, they hand it over, you enter it, and escrow opens. Your payout leaves to your bank. Done.

Never hand over the item before the buyer gives you the PIN. The PIN is what releases your money. No PIN, no payout, so do not let the item leave your hands until you have it.
