Buying on DPS

Browse listings, buy at the asking price, and get your item with escrow protection.

+ How do I buy an item?

Find the item you want on Live Items or in the Telegram channel. Click Buy now. Enter your email, pay through Paystack, and we'll send your 4-digit PIN.

Once payment clears, you and the seller get each other's contact details. Coordinate pickup, inspect the item, and only share your PIN once you're satisfied. That's when escrow releases to the seller.

+ How do I know listings are real?

Every listing is reviewed by our team before it goes live. We check the photos, the description, the price, and the seller's history. If something looks off — clearly misleading, missing key detail, or suspiciously priced — it doesn't go up.

That's why DPS prices tend to be higher than unverified marketplaces. What you see is what you get.

+ Can I buy at the asking price, or do I have to negotiate?

Both work. Buy now purchases the item at the listed price — the fastest route. If you want to negotiate, tap Negotiate and submit your offer for a ₦300 fee.

Most people negotiate on higher-priced items. For smaller things, buying at asking is usually quicker and just as safe.

+ What payment methods can I use?

All payments run through Paystack, Nigeria's leading payment processor. You can pay with:

  • Debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Verve)
  • Bank transfer
  • USSD
  • Bank account (pay with bank)

Paystack handles all card data. We never see or store your card details.

+ Can I inspect the item before paying?

Yes — but not before paying. Here's why: the item stays visible to other buyers until payment confirms it. Once escrow holds your funds, the item is reserved for you, and you can inspect it fully at pickup.

If anything doesn't match the listing, you walk away without sharing your PIN. Full refund, no argument.

The PIN is your inspection window. You're not confirming the sale when you pay — you're confirming it after you've seen the item.
+ Do I pay extra fees as a buyer?

No. Buyers pay the agreed price — nothing else. Commission comes out of the seller's payout.

The only additional cost is the ₦300 negotiation fee if you choose to make an offer instead of buying at the asking price. That fee is charged by Paystack at the point of offer.

Offers & negotiation

How to make an offer, what happens next, and why the ₦300 fee exists.

+ How do I make an offer?

Open the item page, tap Negotiate, and enter your offer. Use the ± buttons to move in ₦1,000 steps, or just type the number. Add an optional note, enter your email, and pay the ₦300 fee through Paystack.

The seller gets notified immediately. They have 24 hours to accept, counter, or decline.

+ Why is there a ₦300 fee?

Because it keeps offers serious. Without a fee, marketplaces get flooded with lowball offers from people who aren't actually ready to buy. The ₦300 filters that out.

The fee also covers verification, buyer screening, and the email infrastructure that keeps you informed at every step.

The fee is non-refundable. It stays with the platform whether or not your offer is accepted.
+ What happens after I send an offer?

Three possibilities, and you'll get an email for whichever happens:

  • Accepted. You get a payment link at your offered price. Pay within the window and the item is yours.
  • Countered. The seller comes back with a different price. You have 24 hours to accept, decline, or walk away.
  • Declined or expired. The offer closes. You can try again if the item is still live.
+ Can I withdraw my offer?

Yes. Any time before the seller responds, you can pull your offer from the link in your confirmation email. The seller is notified that you've withdrawn.

The ₦300 fee is not refunded — it covered the submission and verification, which already happened.

+ What if the seller doesn't respond?

The offer expires automatically after 24 hours. You get an email when it does. The item stays live and you can try again with a fresh offer if you want.

Sellers who repeatedly let offers expire without responding get flagged in our system. It affects their visibility on the platform.

+ Can I have two offers on the same item?

No. Only one live offer per buyer per item at a time. If you already have a pending or countered offer, you'll need to respond to it, withdraw it, or wait for it to expire before submitting a new one.

This prevents accidental double-payment and keeps things clean on the seller's side.

Escrow & PIN

How the money stays protected from payment to pickup.

+ How does escrow actually work?

Buyer pays → funds sit with DPS (not the seller) → buyer inspects at pickup → buyer shares PIN → funds release to the seller minus commission.

Your money never moves without your action. Until the PIN is entered, everything is reversible. That's the whole safety layer.

+ What is the PIN and how does it work?

The PIN is a 4-digit code you get by email the moment your payment is confirmed. You share it with the seller at pickup, only after you've inspected and accepted the item.

The seller enters it into the system. That's the release signal. Escrow opens, they get paid, the sale is complete.

+ What if I never share the PIN?

Nothing moves. The money stays in escrow. If the item turned out not to match, you did the right thing — walk away, and contact us to arrange a refund.

If it's just pickup logistics taking time, no problem. Escrow holds until you and the seller are ready.

+ Who can see my PIN?

Only you. It's emailed to the address you paid with, and to nobody else. Not the seller, not even DPS staff.

Anyone asking for your PIN by phone, Telegram, or DM is trying to steal. Report them immediately.
+ Can the money be refunded after I share the PIN?

No. Once the PIN is entered and verified, escrow releases and the transaction is final. That's the entire point of the PIN — it's your last checkpoint.

If a problem surfaces afterward, contact us anyway. We'll look into it, but reversal isn't something we can promise.

Selling on DPS

Listing items, getting approved, and what happens after a sale.

+ How do I list an item?

Tap Sell on the site, or send your details to Dayo on Telegram. You'll need:

  • Item name and short description
  • Condition (Brand New, Like New, Good Condition, Fairly Used)
  • Location (Mainland or Island)
  • Asking price in naira
  • Preferred pickup time
  • Up to 5 real photos of the item
  • Your bank details (one-time setup for new sellers)

Listing is free. Takes about two minutes.

+ How long until my item goes live?

Most listings are reviewed within a few hours. We check photos, description, price, and general quality. If everything looks good, it goes live and starts appearing on the marketplace and Telegram channel.

If something needs fixing — blurry photos, vague description — we'll email you with specific notes so you can resubmit.

+ What's the commission?

Individual sellers: 13% commission. You keep 87% of the sale price.

DPS Partners: 5% commission. Partners keep 95%. Partner rates apply after account verification.

Commission is deducted automatically at payout. No upfront fees, no listing fees, no hidden charges.

+ Can I reject an offer?

Absolutely. Every offer comes with three buttons: Accept, Counter, or Decline. You choose.

If you decline, the buyer is notified and the item stays live. If you counter, the buyer has 24 hours to respond to your price. No obligation to accept anything you don't want.

+ What if a buyer never shows up for pickup?

Contact us. We'll reach the buyer and, if needed, extend the escrow window so you're not stuck waiting. Buyers with a pattern of no-shows get flagged and restricted.

The money stays safely in escrow during the delay. You're not out anything.

+ Can I list multiple items at once?

Yes. Sellers can list as many items as they want. Partners can list at scale — including automated import from their Instagram page through Dina.

For high-volume sellers, the Partner Program is worth looking at. Lower commission, bulk tools, and dedicated support.

Payouts

Getting your money, when it arrives, and what to do if something goes wrong.

+ When do I get paid after a sale?

Once the buyer shares their PIN and the sale is confirmed, payout is queued to your bank account. Most payouts land within 24 hours of PIN verification, typically the same day during banking hours.

+ How do I add or change my bank details?

Bank details are collected during your first listing. If they need updating, contact us at info@declutterpawnshop.com from the email on your account. We'll verify and update.

For security, we don't allow self-service bank detail changes. This prevents account takeover from redirecting payouts.

+ What if my bank details are wrong?

If a payout fails because of incorrect bank details, we'll contact you. The money stays in escrow until we get corrected details and re-attempt the payout. You're not at risk of losing the funds.

Please double-check your bank name, account number, and account name when you first set them up.

+ Do I pay tax on my DPS earnings?

That depends on your individual tax situation. DPS doesn't withhold or report on your behalf beyond what's legally required. If you're a regular seller, we recommend keeping records of your payouts and consulting a tax professional.

Transaction records are retained for 7 years and available on request if you need them for accounting.

Partner Program

For businesses moving inventory at scale — better rates, automated listing, dedicated support.

+ What's the Partner Program?

It's DPS for businesses. You list inventory at scale, get a lower commission rate (5% vs 13%), and unlock Dina — our Instagram automation that imports listings directly from your Instagram page.

If you run a declutter page, a thrift store, or any pre-owned business, this is built for you.

+ What is Dina?

Dina is our Instagram scraper and listing bot. Once your account is verified as a Partner, Dina monitors your Instagram page and imports new posts as DPS listings — automatically.

You keep posting on Instagram like normal. Dina handles the rest: pricing, description cleanup, image optimization, and publishing to DPS.

+ How do I become a Partner?

Register at /partner-register. You'll need:

  • Business name and description
  • Instagram handle
  • Email, WhatsApp, and optionally CAC registration
  • Bank account details for payouts

Once verified — usually within a few business days — your Partner ID is issued and Dina activates on your Instagram handle.

+ Is there a monthly fee for Partners?

No. No listing fees, no subscription, no upfront cost. Partner commission (5%) is deducted automatically at payout — same as individual sellers, just a lower rate.

You only pay when you sell.

Safety & disputes

Staying safe at pickup, resolving problems, and what to do if something goes wrong.

+ Where should I meet for pickup?

Public place, daytime, well-lit. A mall, a coffee shop, a busy street corner. Bring a friend if it's a high-value item. Trust your instincts — if something feels off, walk away without sharing your PIN.

+ What if the item doesn't match the listing?

Don't share the PIN. Walk away. Contact us with photos or a description of the discrepancy and we'll process a full refund to your original payment method.

That's exactly the scenario escrow exists for.

+ What if I've already shared my PIN but there's a problem?

Contact us immediately at info@declutterpawnshop.com. Post-confirmation disputes are harder to resolve — escrow has already released — but we'll look into every case individually and take action against bad actors.

If there's evidence of fraud, we'll escalate it seriously.

+ How do I report a suspicious listing or user?

Every listing has a Report this listing link at the bottom. It opens a conversation with Dayo, who forwards it to our team. You can also email us directly.

We investigate every report. Reports of scams, stolen goods, or misrepresentation are handled urgently.
