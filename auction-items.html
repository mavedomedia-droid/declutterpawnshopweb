How the auction works

1New items take early bidsAny open item takes early bids at the asking price or above. Amounts stay private, only the count shows.
2Five bidders open itAt 5 unique bidders it goes live and every bidder so far gets an email. The top bid shows from here on.
3Bidding is one tapYour amount and your email, that is all. We look you up, and your alerts land in your inbox.
4Ten quiet minutes end itEvery new bid resets the 10:00 clock. When it runs out, the highest bid wins.
5The winner pays in 15 minutesPaystack checkout, escrow until pickup with a PIN. A missed window hands it to the next highest bidder.
