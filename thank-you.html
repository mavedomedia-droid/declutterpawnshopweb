How the sale works

From listing to payout.

Here's exactly what happens once your item goes live and a buyer shows interest.

01
A buyer finds your item

From the DPS marketplace, our Telegram channel, or the link you shared with them directly.

02
They buy — or negotiate

They can pay your asking price directly, or send you an offer. When they offer, you get an email and choose: accept, counter, or decline. No offer goes through without your say-so.

03
Payment is held in escrow

The buyer pays DPS, not you. The money sits in escrow — safe for both sides. Neither of you can touch it yet.

04
Details shared — you arrange pickup

Once payment lands in escrow, you and the buyer get each other's contact info. You agree on a time and place to meet. Bring the item, show it in person.

05
PIN releases your payout

The buyer gets a release PIN. Once they've inspected the item and confirmed it's as described, they enter the PIN — escrow opens, and your payout leaves to your bank. Simple. Secure. Done.
