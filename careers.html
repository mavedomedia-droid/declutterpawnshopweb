Open role · 01

Digital Operations Manager

Flexible hours · core 9am–4pm Lagos · fully remote ₦100,000 – ₦150,000 / month Reports to the founder

We're hiring one person to own the daily rhythm of the marketplace. Not data entry, not code, not sales. Our systems handle payments, emails, partner scraping, and listing generation automatically. What they can't do is make decisions — that's the job. If you've run something before, have a marketing instinct, and can wrangle the tools we've built for scale, this role was written for you.

DPS automates the transaction. It does not automate decisions, relationships, or recovery — and those three things are what you'll do all day. You will not be copying data into spreadsheets or writing code. Listings, pricing, payouts, and partner sync all run on their own. Your job is everything the system can't decide.

What you'll actually do

  • Run the daily loop — every morning, work the admin dashboard: approve or reject new listings, check flagged partners, follow up on pickups older than 24 hours, scan the Dina log for sync errors. 30–45 minutes, twice a day.
  • Own the partner relationship — onboard new partners, help them link Telegram, walk them through how Dina picks up posts, weekly check-ins, monthly reviews. Partners who feel seen stay.
  • Resolve the edge cases — buyers whose seller has gone quiet. Buyers who found a scratch after entering the PIN. Partners selling the same item on Instagram and DPS. These are judgment calls, not tickets.
  • Keep quality high — reject stock photos, catch fake listings, spot pricing that doesn't fit. As listings grow, your eye is the filter.
  • Reconcile the numbers — weekly check that every settled payout hit the right bank. Monthly revenue vs. Payouts vs. Paystack. Feed numbers to the accountant.
  • Report what you see — patterns you spot in buyer messages, partner behaviour, and listing quality go straight to the founder. You see the front line better than anyone.

What this role is not

  • Not a support inbox. Most buyer questions are answered by our emails before they ask.
  • Not data entry. Listings, pricing, payouts, and partner sync all run automatically.
  • Not sales. You're not cold-calling partners or chasing revenue targets.
  • Not a stepping stone into engineering. If you want to write code, apply for that instead.

What we're looking for

  • You've run something before A store, a team, a project, a side hustle. Somewhere you had to make calls that didn't have a script.
  • You have a marketing instinct Digital marketing, social media, content, or campaign management. You can spot what makes a listing pop and what kills it.
  • You handle tools built for scale CRMs, analytics dashboards, ad platforms, automation tools, Notion, Slack, Zapier — you've used systems that do more than a spreadsheet, and you can learn a new one in a day.
  • You're calm under pressure A buyer is upset on a Friday evening. A partner is panicking because their item sold and they can't reach the buyer. You handle it without escalating.
  • You read data You can look at a table of numbers and notice what's off. You don't need SQL, but you can spot a duplicate, an outlier, or a stale record.
  • You write well Messages to partners, WhatsApp to buyers, a short report to the founder. Clear, warm, and precise.
  • You live in Lagos and work remotely You have a laptop, reliable internet, and can keep a fixed 9–4 schedule with a break. No in-person requirement — the job is fully remote, but we value someone who could show up if it ever really mattered.
